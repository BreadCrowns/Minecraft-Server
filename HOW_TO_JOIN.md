## 📱 Mobile (iPhone, iPad & Android)

### Method 1: The Friends Tab (Easiest)
1. Open Minecraft on your phone or tablet and sign into your free Microsoft/Xbox account.
2. Go to **Play** ➔ **Friends** tab ➔ tap **Add Friend**.
3. Search for **`MCBazserv`** and add it as a friend.
4. Go back to the **Friends** tab. Look under **"Joinable Friends"** and tap **MCBazserv** to jump in!

### Method 2: The Direct Server Tab
1. Open Minecraft ➔ tap **Play** ➔ tap the **Servers** tab.
2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap **Add Server**.
3. Enter the following:
   * **Server Name:** `Baz Minecraft Server`
   * **Server Address:** `99.105.59.82` *(or `192.168.1.191` if you are connected to our home Wi-Fi)*
   * **Port:** `19132` *(leave as default)*
4. Tap **Save** and then tap **Play**!

## ❓ Troubleshooting & Tips

* **"I added MCBazserv, but I don't see it under Friends!"**
  * Give it 1–2 minutes after adding to sync on Xbox Live. 
  * If it doesn't appear, close Minecraft completely and reopen it.
* **"Failed to connect to world / Unable to connect":**
  * Check your internet connection.
  * On consoles (PlayStation/Switch/Xbox), ensure your online gaming subscription (PS Plus, Switch Online, or Game Pass Core) is active.
