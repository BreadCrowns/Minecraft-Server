📱 Mobile (iPhone, iPad & Android)

Option A: The Friends Tab (Easiest)

  1. Open Minecraft on your phone or tablet and sign into your free Microsoft/Xbox account.
  2. Go to Play ➔ Friends tab ➔ tap Add Friend.
  3. Search for MCBazserv and add it as a friend.
  4. Go back to the Friends tab. Under Joinable Friends, tap MCBazserv to jump in!

Option B: The Direct Server Tab

  1. Open Minecraft ➔ tap Play ➔ tap the Servers tab.
  2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap Add Server.
  3. Enter the server details:
    • Server Name: Baz Minecraft Server
    • Server Address: 99.105.59.82 (or 192.168.1.191 on our home Wi-Fi)
    • Port: 19132 (default)
  4. Tap Save and tap Play!

🎮 Xbox (Xbox One, Series X | S)

  1. Press the Xbox Guide button on your controller.
  2. Go to People ➔ Find someone.
  3. Search for Gamertag: MCBazserv and select Add Friend.
  4. Launch Minecraft and select Play.
  5. Move over to the Friends tab.
  6. Under Joinable Friends, select MCBazserv to join the server!

(If you are visiting our house on our home Wi-Fi, the server will also automatically show up under "LAN Games" at the bottom of the Friends tab!)

🎮 PlayStation (PS4 & PS5)

(Requires an active PlayStation Plus subscription for online multiplayer).

  1. Open Minecraft on your PlayStation.
  2. Ensure you are signed into your free Microsoft account (check the bottom-left corner of the main menu).
  3. Select Play ➔ move to the Friends tab.
  4. Click Find Cross-Platform Friends (or Add Friend).
  5. Type in the gamertag: MCBazserv and send a friend request.
  6. Return to the Friends tab. Under Joinable Cross-Platform Friends, select MCBazserv to connect!

🕹️ Nintendo Switch

(Requires an active Nintendo Switch Online membership for online multiplayer).

  1. Open Minecraft on your Switch.
  2. Make sure you are signed into your free Microsoft account.
  3. Select Play ➔ move to the Friends tab.
  4. Select Find Cross-Platform Friends.
  5. Search for gamertag: MCBazserv and add it.
  6. Return to the Friends tab, find MCBazserv under your joinable friends list, and select it to join!
